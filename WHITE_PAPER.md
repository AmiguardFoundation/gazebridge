# 🧬 GazeBridge White Paper

**Digital Neuro-Orthotic for Oculomotor Apraxia**

### 1. The Innovation: Inhibition-as-Input

GazeBridge flips the paradigm. Instead of tracking eye movement, we measure the **biomechanical stability** of the user.
The content unlocks only when the "Neural Lock" (800ms of stillness) is achieved.

### 2. Edge AI: Gemma 4

We use **Gemma 4** to process linguistic data locally. This ensures:

1. **Privacy:** Data never leaves the device.
   
2. **Speed:** Instant "Word Equations" generation.

### 3. **The Matryoshka Effect (Sequential Unlocking)**
To eliminate "Visual Crowding," GazeBridge utilizes a hierarchical reveal system:

* **Layer 1 (The Shell):** A simple visual anchor (Icon/Shape).
* **Layer 2 (The Frame):** The root morpheme (e.g., "PLAY").
* **Layer 3 (The Core):** The full semantic meaning (e.g., "PLAY + GROUND").

This ensures the Magnocellular pathway is never overwhelmed. The brain processes one "door" before the next one opens.
